Lesson 0: Addition with Blocks

Before multiplication, let's make sure addition is rock solid. We'll use blocks so every fact is something you can actually see and count — not just remember. We'll go one number at a time, starting with the biggest, 9, then 8, then 7, all the way down to 1.

What the blocks show you

9 + 4 means 9 blocks and 4 more blocks, all pushed together. Count every block and that's your answer.

9 blue blocks, 4 orange blocks, 13 blocks total. That's 9 + 4 = 13.

Tip: the blocks are grouped in rows of 5, so you can count by 5s instead of one at a time.
