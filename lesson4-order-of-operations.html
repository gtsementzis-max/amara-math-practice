Lesson 4 · Order of operations (PEMDAS)

The Midnight Potion Order

Every midnight potion must be brewed by adding its ingredients in the exact right order, or it fizzles into swamp sludge. Math expressions work the same way — there's an exact order for solving them, called PEMDAS.

🔮 The Four Potion Rules

Please Excuse My Dear Aunt Sally — that's the old memory spell for the order: Parentheses, Exponents, Multiply/Divide, Add/Subtract.
P · Parentheses

Solve everything inside the ( ) first — cast the circle before anything else.

E · Exponents

Solve any power next. For example, 3² means 3 × 3 = 9 — charge the crystal.

MD · Multiply & Divide

Next, working left to right, do every × and ÷ in the order they appear — swirl the potion. × and ÷ are the same rank, so whichever comes first (left to right) goes first.

AS · Add & Subtract

Last, working left to right, do every + and − — the final stir. + and − are the same rank too, so left to right wins.
