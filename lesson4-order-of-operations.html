Lesson 4 · Order of operations (PEMDAS)

The Midnight Potion Order

Every midnight potion must be brewed by adding its ingredients in the exact right order, or it fizzles into swamp sludge. A math potion recipe works the same way — there's an exact order for solving it, called PEMDAS.

🔮 The Four Potion Rules 1 · Watch 👀

Please Excuse My Dear Aunt Sally — that's the old memory spell for the order: Parentheses, Exponents, Multiply/Divide, Add/Subtract.
P · Parentheses

Solve everything inside the ( ) first — cast the circle before anything else.

E · Exponents

Solve any power next. For example, 3² means 3 × 3 = 9 — charge the crystal.

The little number up high is a power. It tells you how many times to multiply the big number by itself. 3² = 3 × 3 = 9. 2³ = 2 × 2 × 2 = 8. Careful — 3² is NOT 3 × 2!
MD · Multiply & Divide

Next do every × and ÷, left to right — swirl the potion. × and ÷ are a tie, so whichever comes first goes first.

AS · Add & Subtract

Last, working left to right, do every + and − — the final stir. + and − are a tie too, so left to right wins.
