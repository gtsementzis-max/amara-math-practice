Lesson 6 · Big-number division

The Dragon Hoard Vault

The hoard has too many coins to share one by one. Today Amara learns three ways to split big numbers fast — the rectangle, the box, and the Big 7 ladder — all built from the division she already knows.

🪙 Arrays: find the missing side

12 coins in 3 rows make a rectangle with 4 in each row. 3 × 4 = 12, so 12 ÷ 3 = 4. Division finds the MISSING SIDE of the rectangle.

12 ÷ 3

3 rows … ? in each row → 4

18 ÷ 6

6 rows … ? in each row → 3

Now imagine the rectangle as a BOX: 3 on the side, 12 inside, ? on top. When the number is big we split the box into friendly pieces — that is the next card.

📦 Split the box

Tap through each step. We cut the big number into friendly pieces the divisor can handle, then add up what goes on top.

🐉 The Big 7 ladder (partial quotients)

Same boxes, written as a ladder: subtract a chunk you know, write how many groups it was, and keep going until you cannot subtract any more. Add up the groups.

Example 1 of 3

🏴‍☠️ Raid the hoard: take your own chunks

Tap a chunk to take that many groups out of the hoard. Take 10s, 5s, 2s or 1s in any order you like — if a chunk is too big, the hoard will tell you. When fewer coins are left than the divisor, you are done.
