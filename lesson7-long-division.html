🐉 The four steps: Dragons Munch Sweet Berries

D · Divide

How many times does the divisor go into the number you are looking at? Write that digit on top.

M · Multiply

Multiply that digit by the divisor. Write the answer underneath.

S · Subtract

Subtract to see what is left. It must be SMALLER than the divisor — if not, your D digit was too small.

B · Bring down

Bring down the next digit and start again at D.

The classic mnemonic is Does McDonald's Sell Burgers. The dragon version is Dragons Munch Sweet Berries — same four steps.

🧭 Six ways to see division

You now know SIX ways to solve the same division. Here is 52 ÷ 4 done every way — they all agree.

1. Equal groups

Share 52 coins into 4 chests: 13 in each.

2. Sharing or grouping

Sharing: 52 shared into 4 chests → 13 each.

Grouping: how many 4s fit in 52? → 13 groups.

Same answer, two questions.

3. Array and box

4 rows of 13 = 52, so the missing side is 13.

4. Number line

Hop back by 4 from 52: 13 hops, land on 0.

5. Big 7 ladder

Take 10 groups (40), then 3 groups (12): 13.

6. The scroll

Divide, Multiply, Subtract, Bring down.

All six say 52 ÷ 4 = 13. Use the one that makes sense to you — a Dragon Master knows them all.
